INTRODUCING OUR MATERIAL CONCEPT

Meet THERMOWEAVE.

A proposed thermal-insulation material concept that brings recovered textile fibres into the built environment.

CONCEPT / 001FIBRE. FORM. FUNCTION.
FROM WASTE STREAM TO BUILDING MATERIAL

Rethinking the
layers around us.

Our concept explores how textile fibres, compressed with a suitable binder, could be developed into insulation panels for building applications.

  • 01 Recovered textile fibres
  • 02 Material processing and binding
  • 03 Panel formats for building applications

THERMOWEAVE is at the idea and research stage. Performance, safety, durability and cost claims require prototype testing and independent validation.

04 WHY THERMORA

We believe better materials can help shape better everyday living.

THERMORA is an emerging idea built around a simple question: what if materials considered waste could help address a real need in our built environment?

We’re at the beginning of the journey — learning, researching and working towards a solution that can be tested in the real world.
